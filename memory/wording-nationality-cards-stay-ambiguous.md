# Keep the nationality cards ambiguous in Japanese

* Type: Wording
* Added: 2026-11-02
* Source: a 2026-11-02 Claude Code session that added `q25` to `q27` to `src/data/questions.ts`; the owner chose "Keep it ambiguous" over two readings that name who judged whom

`q25` ("Not Japanese enough") and `q26` ("Not foreign enough") do not say who was not enough.
The card could mean that a date judged the player, or that the player judged a date.
The owner chose to keep that open in Japanese as well, so the labels are `日本人らしさが足りない` and `外国人らしさが足りない`.
These follow the short noun-phrase style of `外国人フェチ` (`q03`).

Why it matters: AGENTS.md allows the Japanese labels to be refined.
A refinement that adds a subject, such as `と言われた` or `相手が`, would pick one reading and change the joke.

How to apply: when you polish these two labels, keep them free of a subject or a speaker.
Ask the owner before you commit to one reading.
